# Cinch

A four-player, partnership trick-taking card game for iPhone — the one your family
probably plays a little differently than everyone else's.

Play solo against three opponents, or pass the phone around the table for four. It will
teach you the rules by dealing you real hands, including the odd ones that make Cinch
what it is.

Cinch runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no network
required to play.
