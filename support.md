# Support

## Getting help

Email **cinchcardgame@gmail.com** with questions, problems, or rules disputes. Please
include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting something going wrong.

If you are testing Cinch through TestFlight, the fastest route is TestFlight's built-in
feedback button — it attaches a screenshot and your device details automatically.

## Common questions

### The rules aren't the way my family plays

Very likely, and we would like to hear about it. Cinch is a family game and the details
drift from table to table.

Three rules in particular were judgement calls, and we are interested in whether yours
differ:

- When both teams tie on Game points, the point goes to nobody.
- The deal rotates left every hand, including after a hand where everybody passed.
- Scores can go below zero.

The game also plays to 21, deals six-card hands, and treats a Cinch as an eleven-point
swing. If any of that is not how you learned it, please say so.

### A score looks wrong

At the end of each hand, Cinch counts High, Low, Jack and Game out in front of you rather
than simply announcing a total. If a number still looks wrong, take a screenshot of that
review screen and send it — that screen shows the working, so it is usually enough to
settle the question either way.

### Does my game save if I close the app?

Yes. A game in progress is saved on your device and offered back when you return.

### Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Cinch is fully playable offline.

### What happened to my player names?

Names you type stay on your device and are never transmitted. They are not backed up to
any server, so reinstalling the app clears them.

## Privacy

See the [privacy policy](privacy). The short version: the names you type never leave your
device, there is no account, and nothing is sold, shared with data brokers, or used for
advertising.
